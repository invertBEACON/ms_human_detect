METADATA
Data and analysis code for White et al. (2026) "Chromatic contrasts predict
human target detection in a natural search task."

Study overview
---------------
Fifty-one volunteers walked a 100 m route through a lightly shaded strip of
trees (bark-mulch background) on the Macquarie University campus, Sydney,
Australia, over five sessions between 5 and 12 May 2017 (trials 11:00-14:00).
Each participant encountered 56 targets deployed along both sides of the route
(28 per side) and verbally reported the colour and side of any they detected.
Targets were seven types: yellow, green and blue stimuli each in a "light"
(single-layer) and "dark" (double-layer) variant, plus a brown control. This
gives 2,856 target-presentations in total (51 x 56).


FILES
=====

data/data_detections.csv
------------------------
One row per target-presentation (2,856 rows). Columns:

  date         Session date (D/M/YYYY). Five sessions, 5-12 May 2017.
  part_id      Participant identifier (p1-p51). Random effect in the model.
  sex          Participant sex: f = female, m = male.
  time         Time of day the trial began, in decimal hours (11.04-13.53).
  weather      Sky condition recorded at the start of the walk:
                 su = sunny, sh = shady, oc = overcast, ps = partly sunny.
  irrad_a      Identifier linking the trial to a folder of raw irradiance
               spectra in data/irrad/ (ambient light measurement). NA where no
               matching measurement is available. Two records
               (i00_0032, i00_0089) are treated as outliers and set to NA in
               the analysis script.
  irrad_b      Second irradiance measurement identifier (largely NA);
               irradiance was measured both outside and within the forest patch.
  distance_m   Lateral distance of the target from the route centreline, in
               metres (2, 3, 4 or 5).
  treatment    Target type (7 levels): G, B, Y = green, blue, yellow (dark);
               GL, BL, YL = the "light" variants; C = brown control.
  colour       Target hue collapsed across lightness: G, B, Y, or C (control).
  lightness    Target lightness variant: low (dark/double-layer) or
               high (light/single-layer). Control targets coded low.
  order        Position of the target in the presentation sequence (1-28).
  side         Side of the route the target was placed: left or right.
  detected     Response: 1 = target detected, 0 = not detected. Model response.

data/target_specs.csv
---------------------
Reflectance spectra of the seven target types plus the background, measured by
spectrophotometry. Column wl is wavelength in nm; remaining columns are the
per-type reflectance (proportion, 0-1): GL, G, BL, B, YL, Y, C (targets) and
bkg (bark-mulch background). Read into the analysis with pavo::as.rspec over
300-700 nm.

data/irrad/
-----------
Raw downwelling irradiance spectra of ambient skylight, measured with a
calibrated OceanView JAZ spectrometer (absolute irradiance module). Organised
in subfolders (e.g. i00_, i01_) whose names correspond to the irrad_a
identifiers in data_detections.csv; each subfolder holds one or more
OUTPUTFILE####.JazIrrad files. Loaded with pavo::getspec(ext = "JazIrrad").

R/01_analysis.R
---------------
Full analysis script: data cleaning and merging, visual modelling (CIELCh /
CIE2000 target-background contrasts via pavo; segment analysis of irradiance),
the binomial GLMM (lme4) predicting detection from hue, chroma and luminance
contrasts plus distance and ambient-light chromaticity with participant as a
random effect, and figure generation.

figs/
-----
Output figures generated by the analysis code.


Derived model variables (created in 01_analysis.R, not stored in the CSVs)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  L_diff        Target-background difference in CIELCh lightness (luminance).
  C_diff        Target-background difference in chroma (saturation).
  h_diff        Target-background difference in hue.
  cie2000       CIEDE2000 colour distance between target and background.
  irrad_chroma  Chromaticity of ambient light (segment-analysis chroma).
  irrad_bright  Brightness of ambient light (segment-analysis measure).
